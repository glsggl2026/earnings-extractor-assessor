Commentary

"INNOVATE executed on several key strategic priorities in the third quarter," said Avie Glazer, Chairman of INNOVATE. “DBM Global capped its final quarter under our ownership with exceptional performance, and we took two significant steps to reshape our portfolio through the sale of a controlling interest in HC2 Broadcasting and, shortly after quarter-end, the sale of DBM Global to IES. These transactions mark meaningful progress in our efforts to unlock the value of our assets, generate funds to help address coming debt repayment and redemption obligations, and further simplify INNOVATE's portfolio and capital structure."

"Our focus now turns to the opportunities within our remaining portfolio, addressing liquidity and continuing to maximize value for INNOVATE shareholders," said Paul Voigt, Interim CEO of INNOVATE. “MediBeacon continues to build commercial momentum and advance its commercialization efforts in the U.S. and China, while HC2 Broadcasting is actively pursuing initiatives to unlock greater potential for its spectrum assets. We are encouraged by the progress and remain focused on supporting their strategic initiatives."

Third Quarter 2026 and Recent Highlights
•As previously announced, on October 5, 2026, INNOVATE completed the sale of DBM Global, Inc. ("DBMG") to IES Holdings, Inc. ("IES") for cash and stock consideration. INNOVATE intends to use all net proceeds from the transaction to reduce its outstanding debt. See INNOVATE's 8-K filed on October 5, 2026, for additional information.
•As previously announced, on September 1, 2026, INNOVATE completed the sale of its controlling interest in HC2 Broadcasting Holdings Inc. (“HC2 Broadcasting”) to CONX CORP. (“CONX”). CONX now owns 75% of HC2 Broadcasting, while INNOVATE retains a 25% ownership interest. See INNOVATE's 8-K filed on September 2, 2026, for additional information.
Infrastructure
•On October 5, 2026, INNOVATE completed the previously announced sale of DBMG to IES. Consideration received by INNOVATE and DBM Global Intermediate Holdco Inc. (together, “Seller”) consisted of Seller’s approximately 91.21% pro rata share of the base purchase price, as adjusted at closing based on estimates of cash, working capital, indebtedness and transaction expenses of DBMG and certain additional adjustments as set forth in the DBMG transaction agreement. The purchase price remains subject to finalization following delivery of a post-closing statement and, if necessary, resolution of any disputes through an independent accounting firm. At closing, Seller received (subject to post-closing adjustments):
◦approximately $378 million in cash, representing Seller’s portion of the $510 million cash consideration after giving effect to Seller’s receipt of 100% of the IES common stock issued as part of the total consideration and closing adjustments; and
◦430,974 shares of IES common stock (as adjusted for IES’s two-for-one stock split effected on August 21, 2026). The stock consideration is subject to a maximum 60-day lock-up period following the closing of the DBMG transaction, subject to the terms of the DBMG transaction agreement.
◦In addition, Seller received $35 million in cash at closing as compensation for costs and obligations to be borne by Seller in connection with the joint tax election under Section 338 of the Internal Revenue Code being made with respect to the DBMG transaction. Including this payment, Seller received total cash of approximately $413 million at closing.
◦The DBMG transaction resulted in repayment or other payment obligations under certain instruments, including the Company’s revolving line of credit, 10.50% Senior Secured Notes due 2027 and 9.50% Convertible Senior Secured Notes due 2027. See “Risk Factors – If we fail to consummate sufficient asset sales, or the value of the consideration received from prior sales is less than anticipated, we may be unable to satisfy our debt service obligations and may be unable to continue as a going concern” in the Company’s Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the period ended September 30, 2026.
•DBMG reported third quarter 2026 revenue of $490.6 million, an increase of 45.0%, compared to $338.4 million in the prior year quarter. Net income attributable to INNOVATE was $25.2 million, compared to $8.8 million for the prior year quarter. Adjusted EBITDA increased to $43.6 million from $23.5 million in the prior year quarter.
•DBMG’s reported backlog and adjusted backlog, which takes into consideration awarded but not yet signed contracts, was $2.6 billion and $2.7 billion, respectively, as of September 30, 2026, compared to reported and adjusted backlog of $1.7 billion and $1.8 billion, respectively, as of December 31, 2025.
Life Sciences
•MediBeacon continued to advance commercialization of the TGFRTM System, with engagement expanding to more than 120 U.S. medical institutions. Initial utilization is underway at leading medical centers.
2

•MediBeacon continued to advance reimbursement initiatives, with applications for additional reimbursement codes in 2026 following engagement with Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services ("CMS") as well as key private payors.
•MediBeacon continued its expansion in China, with 600 TGFR Monitors ordered for the market and shipments planned from the fourth quarter of 2026 through second quarter of 2027.
•R2 Technologies, Inc. ("R2") reported third quarter 2026 revenue of $1.2 million.
•R2's demand for the third quarter reached $3.1 million, with backlog of 116 systems globally at the end of the quarter.
•R2 continued to expand its international footprint during the quarter, launching Glacial Rx and Glacial fx in Thailand, while Electronic Instrument and Technology production of Glacial fx remains underway.
•As previously announced, R2 extended the maturity of its secured promissory note with Lancer Capital from August 1, 2026 to December 31, 2026, and R2’s preferred equity was converted to common equity, simplifying its capital structure.
Spectrum
•On September 1, 2026, INNOVATE completed the previously announced sale of its controlling interest in HC2 Broadcasting to CONX. CONX now owns 75% of HC2 Broadcasting, while INNOVATE retains a 25% ownership interest.
•Broadcasting reported third quarter 2026 revenue of $3.7 million, compared to $5.6 million in the prior year quarter. Net income attributable to INNOVATE was $25.9 million, including the gain on sale of $32.9 million, compared to Net loss of $5.9 million in the prior year quarter. Adjusted EBITDA was $0.3 million, compared to $1.0 million in the prior year quarter.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
•Revenue: For the third quarter of 2026, INNOVATE's consolidated revenue was $495.5 million, an increase of 42.8%, compared to $347.1 million for the prior year quarter. The increase was driven primarily by our Infrastructure segment, which was partially offset by a decrease at our Spectrum and Life Sciences segments. The increase at our Infrastructure segment was primarily driven by the timing and size of projects at DBMG's commercial structural steel fabrication and erection business, which had increased activity subsequent to the comparable period on certain large construction projects, as well as an increase at our industrial maintenance and repair business due to the timing and size of projects. The decrease at our Spectrum segment was primarily driven by our disposition of a controlling interest in our Spectrum segment, as discussed above, which resulted in two months of consolidated Spectrum revenue in the current period compared to three months in the comparable period. The decrease at our Life Sciences segment was attributable to R2, reflecting decreases in unit sales across all product lines and regions, due to liquidity constraints.

•Adjusted EBITDA: For the third quarter of 2026, Total Adjusted EBITDA was $40.5 million compared to Total Adjusted EBITDA of $19.8 million for the prior year quarter. The increase in Adjusted EBITDA was primarily driven by our Infrastructure, Life Sciences and Non-Operating Corporate segments, which were partially offset by a decrease at our Spectrum segment. The increase at our Infrastructure segment was primarily driven by an increase in revenue and gross profit at DBMG's commercial structural steel fabrication and erection business, which had increased activity subsequent to the comparable period on certain large construction projects, combined with changes in the estimate of the cost to complete those projects recognized in the ordinary course driven by efficiencies recognized around certain projects. The increase was partially offset by an increase in recurring SG&A expenses, primarily driven by the timing of, and increases in compensation-related expenses and an increase in professional fees, as well as a decrease in gross profit at our industrial maintenance and repair business, despite the increase in revenue discussed above, primarily due to fluctuations in material costs. The increase at our Life Sciences segment was primarily driven by a decrease in compensation-related expenses at R2, which was partially offset by a decrease in gross profit at R2, driven by the decrease in revenue, and an increase in equity method losses recognized from MediBeacon. The increase at our Non-Operating Corporate segment was primarily driven by a decrease in professional fees. The decrease in Adjusted EBITDA at our Spectrum segment was primarily driven by our disposition of a controlling interest in our Spectrum segment, as explained above, which resulted in two months of consolidated Spectrum activity in the current period compared to three months in the comparable period.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
In this press release, INNOVATE refers to certain financial measures that are not presented in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“U.S. GAAP”), including Total Adjusted EBITDA (excluding discontinued operations, if applicable) and Adjusted EBITDA for its operating segments. In addition, other companies may define Adjusted EBITDA differently than we do, which could limit its usefulness.
Adjusted EBITDA
Management believes that Adjusted EBITDA provides investors with meaningful information for gaining an understanding of our results as it is frequently used by the financial community to provide insight into an organization’s operating trends and facilitates comparisons between peer companies, since interest, taxes, depreciation, amortization and the other items listed in the definition of Adjusted EBITDA below can differ greatly between organizations as a result of differing capital structures and tax strategies. Adjusted EBITDA can also be a useful measure of a company’s ability to service debt. While management believes that non-U.S. GAAP measurements are useful supplemental information, such adjusted results are not intended to replace our U.S. GAAP financial results. Using Adjusted EBITDA as a performance measure has inherent limitations as an analytical tool as compared to net income (loss) or other U.S. GAAP financial measures, as this non-U.S. GAAP measure excludes certain items, including items
6

that are recurring in nature, which may be meaningful to investors. As a result of the exclusions, Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered in isolation and does not purport to be an alternative to net income (loss) or other U.S. GAAP financial measures as a measure of our operating performance.

The calculation of Adjusted EBITDA, as defined by us, consists of Net income (loss) attributable to INNOVATE Corp., excluding: discontinued operations, if applicable; depreciation and amortization; other operating (income) loss (which is inclusive of (gain) loss on sale or disposal of assets, lease termination costs, (gains) losses on lease modifications, and asset impairment expense); (gain) loss on sale of controlling interest in subsidiary; interest expense; (gain) loss on extinguishment of debt; other (income) expense, net; income tax expense (benefit); non-controlling interests; share-based compensation expense; realignment and exit costs; facility commissioning costs; debt refinancing costs; and acquisition and disposition costs.
