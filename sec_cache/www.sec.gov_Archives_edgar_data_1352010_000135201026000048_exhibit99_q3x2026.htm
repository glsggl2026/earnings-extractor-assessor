EPAM Reports Results for Third Quarter 2026
•Third quarter revenues of $1.426 billion, up 2.3% year-over-year and 2.5% on an organic constant currency basis
•Third quarter GAAP income from operations increased to 12.3% of revenues from 10.4%, and non-GAAP income from operations increased to 16.7% of revenues from 16.0%, on a year-over-year basis
•Third quarter GAAP diluted EPS of $2.53, an increase of $0.62, or 32.5%, and non-GAAP diluted EPS of $3.51, an increase of $0.43, or 14.0%, on a year-over-year basis
•Increased share repurchase authorization by $1.5 billion, bringing the total remaining authorization to $1.8 billion, and plans to enter a $500 million accelerated share repurchase next week
•EPAM commits to return at least 50% of free cash flow to shareholders annually from 2027 through 2029
•For the full year, EPAM now expects the year-over-year revenue growth rate to be in the range of 3.5% to 3.7% and now expects the year-over-year revenue growth rate on an organic constant currency basis to be in the range of 2.5% to 2.7%
•For the full year, EPAM now expects its GAAP diluted EPS to be in the range of $8.41 to $8.49, and non-GAAP diluted EPS to be in the range of $13.23 to $13.31
Newtown, PA, USA — November 5, 2026 — EPAM Systems, Inc. (NYSE: EPAM), a leading digital and AI transformation company, today announced results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“We delivered a strong quarter, with better-than-expected results, continued margin expansion, AI-native momentum and traction in our transformation strategy,” said Balazs Fejes, CEO & President, EPAM. “We continued to scale our strategic partnerships and forward-deployed engineering network, launched our new Frontier AI Services and increased capital return to shareholders.”
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
•Revenues increased to $1.426 billion, a year-over-year increase of $32.1 million, or 2.3%. On an organic constant currency basis, revenues were up 2.5% compared to the third quarter of 2025;
•GAAP income from operations was $175.9 million, an increase of $31.0 million, or 21.4%, compared to $144.9 million in the third quarter of 2025. As a percentage of revenues, GAAP income from operations increased to 12.3% from 10.4% in the third quarter of 2025;
•Non-GAAP income from operations was $238.4 million, an increase of $15.6 million, or 7.0%, compared to $222.8 million in the third quarter of 2025. As a percentage of revenues, non-GAAP income from operations increased to 16.7% from 16.0% in the third quarter of 2025;
•Diluted earnings per share (“EPS”) on a GAAP basis was $2.53, an increase of $0.62, or 32.5%, compared to $1.91 in the third quarter of 2025; and
•Non-GAAP diluted EPS was $3.51, an increase of $0.43, or 14.0%, compared to $3.08 in the third quarter of 2025.








Cash Flow and Other Metrics
•Cash provided by operating activities was $288.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to cash provided by operating activities of $294.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025;
•Cash provided by operating activities was $250.1 million for the first nine months of 2026, compared to cash provided by operating activities of $372.0 million for the first nine months of 2025;
•Cash, cash equivalents and restricted cash totaled $969.9 million as of September 30, 2026, a decrease of $331.4 million, or 25.5%, from $1.301 billion as of December 31, 2025; and
•Total headcount was approximately 63,650 as of September 30, 2026. Included in this number were approximately 57,350 delivery professionals, an increase of 1.2% from June 30, 2026.
Capital Allocation
•EPAM has returned approximately $1.7 billion to shareholders since the initiation of the Company’s repurchase programs in 2023.
•The Company spent approximately $489 million on share repurchases during the first nine months of 2026 under its share repurchase program, which included approximately $80 million during the third quarter.
•During the week of November 9, 2026, EPAM expects to enter into a $500 million accelerated share repurchase (Q4 ASR). The Company intends to fund the share repurchase under the Q4 ASR using cash on hand and borrowings under its existing credit facility.
•The Company is committed to returning at least 50% of its free cash flows to shareholders annually from 2027 through 2029.
•In support of this three-year capital return commitment and the Q4 ASR, the Board of Directors approved up to $1.5 billion1 in incremental repurchase authorization.

2026 Outlook - Full Year and Fourth Quarter
Full Year

EPAM expects the following for the full year:
•The Company now expects the year-over-year revenue growth rate to be in the range of 3.5% to 3.7% for 2026 and now expects the year-over-year revenue growth rate on an organic constant currency basis to be in the range of 2.5% to 2.7%;
•For the full year, EPAM now expects GAAP income from operations to be in the range of 10.7% to 10.9% of revenues and non-GAAP income from operations to be in the range of 15.9% to 16.1% of revenues;
•The Company continues to expect its GAAP effective tax rate to be approximately 27% and its non-GAAP effective tax rate to be approximately 24%; and
•EPAM now expects GAAP diluted EPS to be in the range of $8.41 to $8.49 and non-GAAP diluted EPS to be in the range of $13.23 to $13.31. The Company now expects weighted average diluted shares outstanding for the year to be 52.0 million.
Fourth Quarter
EPAM expects the following for the fourth quarter:
•The Company expects revenues will be in the range of $1.405 billion to $1.420 billion for the fourth quarter, reflecting a year-over-year change of (0.2)% to 0.9%. The Company expects the year-over-year revenue growth rate on an organic constant currency basis to be 0.5% to 1.6%;
•For the fourth quarter, EPAM expects GAAP income from operations to be in the range of 11.5% to 12.5% of revenues and non-GAAP income from operations to be in the range of 16.0% to 17.0% of revenues;
•The Company expects its GAAP effective tax rate to be approximately 26% and its non-GAAP effective tax rate to be approximately 24%; and
•EPAM expects GAAP diluted EPS will be in the range of $2.43 to $2.51 for the quarter, and non-GAAP diluted EPS will be in the range of $3.50 to $3.58 for the quarter. The Company expects weighted average diluted shares outstanding for the quarter to be 49.9 million.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
EPAM supplements results reported in accordance with United States generally accepted accounting principles, referred to as GAAP, with non-GAAP financial measures. Management believes these measures help illustrate underlying trends in EPAM’s business and uses the measures to establish budgets and operational goals, communicate internally and externally, for managing EPAM’s business and evaluating its performance. Management also believes these measures help investors compare EPAM’s operating performance with its results in prior periods. EPAM anticipates that it will continue to report both GAAP and certain non-GAAP financial measures in its financial results, including non-GAAP results that exclude stock-based compensation expenses, acquisition-related costs including amortization of acquired intangible assets, impairment of assets, expenses associated with EPAM's humanitarian commitment to its professionals in Ukraine, employee separation costs incurred in connection with restructuring programs, certain other one-time charges and benefits, changes in fair value of contingent consideration, foreign exchange gains and losses, excess tax benefits and tax shortfalls related to stock-based compensation, and the related effect on income taxes of the pre-tax adjustments. Management also compares revenues on an “organic constant currency basis,” which is a non-GAAP financial measure. This measure excludes the effect of acquisitions by removing revenues from an acquired company in the twelve months after completing an acquisition and foreign currency exchange rate fluctuations by translating current period revenues into U.S. dollars at the weighted average exchange rates of the prior period of comparison. Because EPAM’s reported non-GAAP financial measures are not calculated in accordance with GAAP, these measures are not comparable to GAAP and may not be comparable to similarly described non-GAAP measures reported by other companies within EPAM’s industry. Consequently, EPAM’s non-GAAP financial measures should not be evaluated in isolation or supplant comparable GAAP measures, but rather, should be considered together with the information in EPAM’s consolidated financial statements, which are prepared in accordance with GAAP.
