United Community Banks, Inc. Reports Third Quarter Earnings

Third Quarter Results Reflect Execution of Significant Strategic Initiatives, Strong Core Loan Growth, and Robust Deposit Growth

 

GREENVILLE, SC – October 20, 2026 – United Community Banks, Inc. (NYSE: UCB) (United) today announced financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, which included 9.8% annualized core loan growth, 8.0% annualized core customer deposit growth, and a stable margin following six consecutive quarters of margin expansion.

 

Chairman and CEO Lynn Harton stated, “Our third quarter results reflect key strategic actions that simplify our balance sheet, reduce risk, and strengthen future operating performance. We completed the acquisition of Peach State Bank on August 1, the sale of our Navitas equipment finance business on September 1, and the restructuring of our investment portfolio in early September. The restructuring shortened duration and significantly improved the yield on our portfolio. At the same time, we achieved high single-digit growth in both core loans and customer deposits in the quarter, reflecting strong momentum resulting from our investment in hiring top talent. New loan production exceeded $1 billion for the second consecutive quarter. The strategies executed in the third quarter position us very well for the future, as demonstrated by improvements in operating return on assets, return on tangible common equity, and operating efficiency ratio.”

 

In the third quarter, United sold its Navitas equipment finance business for $2.0 billion in cash proceeds, a 7% premium to par. Following the sale, $2.2 billion in held-to-maturity securities were reclassified to available-for-sale designation and long-dated, low yielding securities with an amortized cost basis of $2.5 billion were subsequently sold. The securities repositioning resulted in $2.1 billion of cash proceeds, which, together with the $2.0 billion of Navitas sale proceeds, were redeployed into higher-yielding securities and used to pay down borrowings. Additionally, United completed the acquisition of Peach State Bancshares in the third quarter, increasing density in a dynamic growth market through a complementary, low-risk transaction.

 

Harton continued, “The actions completed in the third quarter allow us to focus on growing the core banking business within our outstanding Southeast footprint. We believe these actions, including our strong liquidity and capital, position us well for long-term success.”

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights:

 

·EPS of $(1.16) on a GAAP basis and $0.77 on an operating basis, compared to $0.95 and $0.71, respectively, in the second quarter of 2026. GAAP EPS reflects strategic actions executed in the third quarter to improve the long-term company performance, including the completion of the sale of our equipment finance business and repositioning of our investment securities portfolio.

 

 

 

 

·Net loss of $139.7 million reflects strategic actions taken during the third quarter. Adjusting for those items, operating net income was $93.7 million in the third quarter, up 8% from the second quarter.
·Total revenue of $(12.0) million resulted from the strategic actions taken in the third quarter. Adjusted for the gain from the sale of Navitas and the loss on the investment portfolio restructuring, which net to $304 million, total operating revenue was $292 million, up from $279 million in the second quarter.
·Net interest margin of 3.68% increased by 10 basis points year-over-year and was stable from the second quarter of 2026.
·Provision for credit losses totaled $10.0 million, including net charge-offs of $4.0 million, or 0.09% of loans for the third quarter.
oAllowance for credit losses coverage was 1.05% of total loans, up one basis point from June 30, 2026.
·Noninterest expense increased $2.3 million on a GAAP basis and decreased $3.3 million on an operating basis, compared to the second quarter.
·Third quarter income taxes reflect the specific tax rates applicable to the Navitas sale and investment securities losses, as well as a higher core effective tax rate resulting from higher estimated pre-tax income and certain discrete items recognized in the quarter.
·GAAP efficiency ratio is not meaningful due to the securities losses incurred in the third quarter. Operating efficiency ratio improved to 53.1% from 56.7% in the second quarter.
·Loan growth of $444 million, or 9.8% annualized, from the second quarter, excluding the impact of Peach State and Navitas.
·Customer deposits were up $473 million, or 8.0% annualized, from the second quarter, excluding deposits received from the Peach State acquisition.
·Peach State contributed $520 million of loans and $678 million of deposits at the time of the acquisition.
·Return on assets was (1.89%) on a GAAP basis and 1.27% on an operating basis. Operating return on assets improved 5 bps from the second quarter.
·Return on common equity on a GAAP basis and return on tangible common equity on an operating basis were (14.8%) and 13.9%, respectively.
·Repurchased 1.395 million common shares at an average price of $35.81 per share for an aggregate of $50 million in the third quarter. The Board also approved an incremental $100 million share repurchase authorization through the end of 2027, bringing the current outstanding repurchase authorization to $113 million.
·Maintained strong capital ratios with preliminary Common Equity Tier 1 of 13.0%.
·Quarterly common dividend increased to $0.26 per share declared during the quarter, up 4% year over year.

 

Year to Date 2026 Financial Highlights:

 

·Net income of $60.2 million, lowered by strategic actions taken in the third quarter. Net income – operating of $265 million, compared with $249 million in 2025.
·Diluted earnings per share of $0.49, or $2.18 on an operating basis compared with operating earnings per share of $2.00 in 2025.

·Pre-tax, pre-provision return on assets of 1.76% on an operating basis up from 1.70% operating pre-tax, pre-provision return on assets for 2025.

About United Community Banks, Inc.

 

United Community Banks, Inc. (NYSE: UCB) is the financial holding company for United Community Bank (United Community), a top-100 U.S. financial institution committed to building stronger communities and improving the financial health and well-being of its customers. United Community offers a full range of banking, mortgage and wealth management services. As of September 30, 2026, United had $28.9 billion in assets and operated 202 offices across Alabama, Florida, Georgia, North Carolina, South Carolina and Tennessee. The company also manages a nationally recognized SBA lending franchise, extending its reach to businesses across the country. United Community is the most awarded bank in the Southeast for Retail Banking Customer Satisfaction by J.D. Power, earning more awards than any other bank in the region, including recognition in 12 of the last 17 years. The company has also been named one of the “Best Banks to Work For” by American Banker for nine consecutive years. In commercial banking, United Community earned multiple 2026 Greenwich Best Bank awards for Small Business Banking. Forbes has consistently named United Community among the World’s Best and America’s Best Banks. Learn more at ucbi.com.
