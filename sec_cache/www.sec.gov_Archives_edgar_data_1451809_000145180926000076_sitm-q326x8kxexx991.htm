SiTime Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Q3 Net Revenue Increased 257% to $298.7 Million
SANTA CLARA, Calif., November 4, 2026 – SiTime Corporation, (Nasdaq: SITM), the Precision Timing company, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Net revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was $298.7 million, as compared to $83.6 million in the year ago quarter.
“With its rapid organic growth and the addition of TPD, SiTime is now operating at a new level of scale and financial performance,” said Rajesh Vashist, chairman and CEO of SiTime. “In Q3, growth was broad-based, demonstrating strength well beyond any single market. Simultaneously, core CED revenue grew 202% year over year as the AI infrastructure buildout continued to drive demand for differentiated timing. TPD integration has gone exceptionally well across our teams, customers and roadmap. We exited the quarter with an annualized revenue run rate above $1 billion and substantial opportunity still ahead.”
In the third quarter of 2026, GAAP gross profit was $154.1 million, or 51.6% of revenue, GAAP operating expenses were $145.8 million, GAAP income from operations was $8.3 million, and GAAP net income was $4.4 million, or $0.14 per diluted share.
In the third quarter of 2026, non-GAAP gross profit was $211.1 million, or 70.7% of revenue, non-GAAP operating expenses were $81.6 million, non-GAAP income from operations was $129.5 million and non-GAAP net income was $130.3 million, or $4.01 per diluted share.
Total cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments were $324.3 million on September 30, 2026.
SiTime plans to discuss its business outlook as part of today’s scheduled conference call.
SiTime also announced a three-for-one forward stock split of SiTime’s issued common stock to make stock ownership more accessible to employees and investors. The split will be effected through an amendment to SiTime’s Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation, which will result in a proportionate increase in the number of shares of authorized common stock. Each record holder of common stock as of the close of market on Thursday, December 3, 2026, will receive two additional shares of common stock for each one share of common stock owned, to be distributed after the close of market on Friday, December 4, 2026. Trading is expected to commence on a split-adjusted basis at market open on Monday, December 7, 2026.
Use of Non-GAAP Financial Information
This press release and its attachments include certain non-GAAP supplemental performance measures. The presentation of this financial information is not intended to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for the financial information prepared and presented in accordance with GAAP.
SiTime believes that the presentation of non-GAAP financial measures provides important supplemental information to management and investors regarding financial and business trends relating to SiTime’s financial condition and results of operations. SiTime believes that these non-GAAP financial measures provide additional insight into SiTime’s ongoing performance and core operational activities and has chosen to provide these measures for more consistent and meaningful comparison between periods. These measures should only be used to evaluate SiTime’s results of operations in conjunction with the corresponding GAAP measures. The non-GAAP financial measures exclude stock-based compensation expense, amortization of acquired intangibles, amortization of inventory step-up costs, amortization of financing related transaction costs, and acquisition-related expenses which include transaction and certain other cash costs associated with business acquisition as well as changes in the estimated fair value of earn out liabilities and accretion of acquisition consideration payable. The reconciliation between GAAP and non-GAAP financial results is provided in the financial statements portion of this release.
Inducement Plan Grants
SiTime granted restricted stock unit awards (“RSUs”) on November 2, 2026 that were approved by the Compensation and Talent Committee of its Board of Directors under SiTime’s Amended and Restated 2022 Inducement Award Plan, as a material inducement to the employment of 71 newly hired individuals globally. The RSUs were approved in accordance with Nasdaq Listing Rule 5635(c)(4). The inducement grants consisted of an aggregate of 47,976 RSUs. For these newly hired individuals, one-fourth of the RSUs will vest on the first February 20, May 20, August 20, or November 20, falling in the one-year anniversary quarter of the applicable vesting start date, and then 1/16th of the RSUs will vest in equal quarterly installments on each February 20, May 20, August 20, and November 20 thereafter, subject to each such employee’s


continued service on each vesting date. The inducement grants are subject to the terms and conditions of award agreements covering the RSU grants and SiTime’s Amended and Restated 2022 Inducement Award Plan.

About SiTime
SiTime is the Precision Timing company. Our people and products help engineers build the impossible. Customers trust SiTime solutions in more than 400 applications, including AI hardware, autonomous vehicles, low Earth orbit satellites and wearables. Built on decades of innovation, our oscillators, clocks, resonators and software deliver higher system performance, greater resilience and better reliability. For more information, visit www.sitime.com.
