Beyond Air Reports Financial Results and Provides Corporate Update

for the Quarter Ended September 30, 2026

 

Record quarterly revenue of $2.3 million, representing approximately 28% year-over-year growth, reflecting continued adoption of first-generation LungFit PH

 

PMA supplement for second-generation LungFit PH system remains under FDA review; transport capability expected to drive greater market penetration, pending approval

 

Reaffirms revenue guidance of $8 million for CY2026 and $16–$18 million for CY2027

 

Conference call today at 4:30 p.m. ET

 

GARDEN CITY, N.Y., November 11, 2026 – Beyond Air, Inc. (NASDAQ: XAIR) (“Beyond Air” or the “Company”), a commercial-stage medical device and biopharmaceutical company focused on harnessing the power of nitric oxide (NO) to improve patients’ lives, today announced financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a corporate update.

 

“Building on the commercial momentum we are generating with our first-generation LungFit PH system, we believe our second-generation system has the potential to substantially expand our market opportunity and accelerate growth, pending FDA approval,” said Robert Goodman, Chief Executive Officer of Beyond Air. “Our US pipeline is now experiencing unprecedented expansion with an acceleration in demand for our first-generation system and high receptivity to our technology and its operational efficiencies, creating a natural demand for our next generation product. We are especially pleased with the pace of new account wins and rate of renewals, which speak to the high level of customer satisfaction with our unique technology – all of which we believe we can leverage into the potential future launch of our second-generation LungFit PH system. Subject to FDA approval, the system is designed to provide a smaller footprint, reduced weight, simplified operation, longer service intervals and expanded operational flexibility. The PMA supplement seeks expanded FDA labeling that would permit use during patient transport by air and ground.”

 

Mr. Goodman continued, “We remain encouraged by our active and productive discussions with the FDA and the progress we are making toward completing the remaining steps in the review process for the second-generation LungFit PH system. We expect to complete our audit-readiness process in mid-December, and subject to FDA inspections and final approval thereafter.”

 

Recent Financial and Operating Highlights

 

● Reported revenue of $2.3 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, the highest quarterly revenue in the Company’s history, representing approximately 28% growth over the same period last year and 31% growth compared with the quarter ended June 30, 2026.
● Executed the Company’s enhanced commercial strategy for first-generation LungFit PH, supporting increased adoption of its cylinder-free nitric oxide technology.
● Advanced preparations for the potential U.S. commercial launch of the second-generation LungFit PH system, subject to FDA approval. If approved, the Company believes the addition of transport capability would enable Beyond Air to serve as a single-source nitric oxide provider for hospitals and health systems.

Financial Results for the Quarter Ended September 30, 2026

 

Revenue for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 was $2.3 million, compared with $1.8 million for the same period last year.

 

Research and development expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were $1.6 million as compared to $2.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease was primarily attributed to lower costs related to the second-generation LungFit PH as the majority of development is complete.

 

Selling, general and administrative expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were $4.6 million as compared to $4.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease was attributed primarily to decreases in stock-based compensation costs, partially offset by increased salaries in our sales organization as we build out our commercial team.

 

Other expense for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $2.2 million as compared to other expense of $0.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase in expense was attributed primarily to an increase in the Company’s outstanding debt and related interest expense, in addition to a charge for scrapped materials.

 

Net loss attributed to common stockholders of Beyond Air, Inc. for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $7.8 million, or a loss of $3.76 per basic and diluted share, compared with a net loss of $7.9 million, or a loss of $24.98 per basic and diluted share, for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

 

As of September 30, 2026, the Company reported cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash and marketable securities of $18.5 million, including $2.7 million of cash held by NeuroNos which is included within current assets held for sale on the balance sheet and $0.7 million of cash held by Beyond Cancer. Restricted cash at September 30, 2026 was $4.8 million.

 

Financial Guidance

 

The Company reaffirms its previously issued revenue guidance of $8 million for calendar year 2026, which excludes revenue from the second-generation LungFit PH system. The Company also reaffirms its revenue guidance of $16 million to $18 million for calendar year 2027, which includes anticipated revenue from the second-generation system, pending FDA approval.

About Beyond Air

 

Beyond Air is a commercial-stage medical device and biopharmaceutical company dedicated to harnessing the power of endogenous and exogenous nitric oxide (NO) to improve the lives of patients suffering from respiratory illnesses, neurological disorders, and solid tumors. The Company has received FDA approval and CE Mark for its first system, LungFit PH, for the treatment of term and near-term neonates with hypoxic respiratory failure. For more information, visit www.beyondair.net.

 

About LungFit

 

Beyond Air’s LungFit is a cylinder-free, phasic flow generator and delivery system designated as a medical device by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA). The ventilator-compatible version of the device can generate NO from ambient air on demand for delivery to the lungs at concentrations ranging from 1 ppm to 80 ppm. The LungFit system could potentially replace large, high-pressure NO cylinders, providing significant advantages in the hospital setting, including greatly reducing inventory and storage requirements, improving overall safety by eliminating NO2 purging steps, and offering other operational benefits.

 

LungFit can also deliver NO at concentrations at or above 80 ppm for potentially treating severe acute lung infections in the hospital setting (e.g., COVID-19, bronchiolitis) and chronic, refractory lung infections in the home setting (e.g., NTM). With the elimination of cylinders, Beyond Air intends to offer NO treatment in the home setting.

 

About Nitric Oxide

 

Nitric Oxide (NO) is a potent molecule, naturally synthesized in the human body, proven to play a critical role in a broad array of biological functions. In the airways, NO targets the vascular smooth muscle cells that surround the small resistance arteries in the lungs. Currently, exogenous inhaled NO is used in adult respiratory distress syndrome, post certain cardiac surgeries and persistent pulmonary hypertension of the newborn to treat hypoxemia. Additionally, NO is believed to play a key role in the innate immune system and in vitro studies suggest that NO possesses anti-microbial activity not only against common bacteria, including both gram-positive and gram-negative, but also against other diverse pathogens.
