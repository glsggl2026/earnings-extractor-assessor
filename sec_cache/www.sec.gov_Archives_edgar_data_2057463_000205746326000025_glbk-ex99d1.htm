LIBERTY CAPITAL REPORTS
THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL AND OPERATING RESULTS

​

Englewood, Colorado, November 5, 2026 – Liberty Capital Corporation (“Liberty Capital”) (Nasdaq: GLIBA, GLIBK) today reported third quarter 2026 results.

​

Headlines include(1):

●For the three months ended September 30, 2026, Liberty Capital(2) revenue increased 4% to $267 million, operating income was $36 million and Adjusted OIBDA(3) increased 10% to $101 million
●Liberty Capital generated net cash provided by operating activities of $311 million and free cash flow(3) of $37 million over the trailing twelve months ended September 30, 2026
●Subsequent to quarter end, GCI raised $300 million of secured financing and retired the remaining 4.75% senior notes due 2028, extending its nearest maturity to 2030
●GCI expects to close the Quintillion acquisition shortly
●Liberty Capital declared a quarterly dividend of $0.375 per share of GCI Group common stock
●Liberty Capital announces a $100 million inaugural share buyback authorization program

​

“We continue to execute against our priorities at GCI and reported a solid quarter that reflects the strength and unmatched reach of our network infrastructure. We expect to close Quintillion soon,” said Ron Duncan, Liberty Capital CEO. “We also declared our first ever quarterly dividend of $0.375 per common share, commencing in December as well as an inaugural $100 million buyback authorization program. These capital allocation initiatives reflect our confidence in the ongoing cash generating power of the business while also preserving our balance sheet capacity for future growth investments.”

​

Corporate Updates

On October 28, 2026, GCI amended its credit agreement, adding a $270 million incremental senior secured Term A-3 loan facility maturing on October 28, 2031, which followed a $30 million increase to its revolving credit facility to $480 million on October 16, 2026. Proceeds from the Term A-3 loan, together with revolver borrowings, were used on October 28, 2026 to fund the full retirement of all outstanding principal amount of GCI’s 4.750% Senior Notes due 2028 at a

1

​

redemption price of 100% of principal plus accrued and unpaid interest. As a result of the retirement, GCI’s earliest maturity was extended to 2030.

​

Today, Liberty Capital announces that its Board of Directors declared a dividend of $0.375 per share of GCI Group common stock, payable on December 15, 2026 to holders of record at the close of business on November 30, 2026. All future dividends remain within the discretion of the Board of Directors (or an authorized committee thereof), and the dividend policy may be modified, suspended or terminated at any time.

​

Today, Liberty Capital announces that its Board of Directors authorized a new share repurchase program, under which Liberty Capital may repurchase up to $100 million of its GCI Group common stock. Repurchases may be made from time to time through open market transactions, privately negotiated transactions or other means. The timing and amount of any future repurchases will be determined based on market conditions, trading price and other factors and the program may be suspended or discontinued at any time.

GCI revenue increased 4% in the third quarter of 2026. Consumer revenue increased 3%, driven by growth in wireless revenue. Business revenue increased 5%, driven by growth in data revenue.

​

GCI revenue was flat for the nine months ended September 30, 2026. Consumer revenue declined 1% due to fully exiting the video business in 2025, partially offset by growth in wireless revenue. Business revenue increased 1% driven by an increase in data revenue.

​

Operating income was $36 million and Adjusted OIBDA increased $9 million to $101 million in the third quarter driven by increased revenue. Operating income was $95 million and Adjusted OIBDA declined $23 million to $290 million for

3

the nine months ended September 30, 2026, driven by increased operating expenses. Operating income in both periods was also impacted by $1 million and $8 million of acquisition costs related to Quintillion, respectively.

​

Year to date, GCI has spent $202 million, net of grant proceeds, on capital expenditures related primarily to improvements to the rural wireless and state-wide data networks in Alaska. GCI's net capital expenditures for the full year 2026 are expected to be $290 million, including $20 million carried over from 2025 due to normal course timing shifts. A significant portion of the increased capital expenditures in 2026 is related to hybrid fiber-coaxial network improvements in urban Alaska.

On a trailing twelve-month basis through the third quarter of 2026, net cash provided by operating activities totaled $311 million and free cash flow over the same period was $37 million.

GCI Consumer revenue increased 3% and declined 1% for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, respectively. The increase during the quarter was driven by growth in wireless revenue. The decrease during the year-to-date period was driven by the impact of fully exiting the video business in 2025, partially offset by growth in wireless revenue.

​

Data revenue increased 2% and declined 1% for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, respectively. The increase for both periods was driven by data revenue from a small broadband provider that GCI acquired during the second quarter, partially offset by an organic decrease in data subscribers. The decline for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was driven by an organic decrease in data subscribers.

​

​

GCI Consumer direct costs increased 3% for the three months ended September 30, 2026 due to an increase in wireless handset costs. Consumer direct costs decreased 5% for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, driven by lower video programming costs from the exit of video services during the third quarter of 2025, partially offset by an increase in wireless handset costs.

NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES

​

SCHEDULE 1

​

To provide investors with additional information regarding our financial results, this press release includes a presentation of Adjusted OIBDA and trailing twelve months of free cash flow, which are non-GAAP financial measures, for Liberty Capital together with reconciliations to operating income and net cash provided by operating activities, respectively, as determined under GAAP, as well as Adjusted OIBDA margin. Liberty Capital defines Adjusted OIBDA as operating income (loss) plus depreciation and amortization, stock-based compensation, separately reported litigation settlements, restructuring, acquisition costs and impairment charges. Liberty Capital defines Adjusted OIBDA margin as Adjusted OIBDA divided by revenue. Liberty Capital defines free cash flow as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditures net of grant proceeds received for capital expenditures.

​

​

Liberty Capital believes Adjusted OIBDA and free cash flow are important indicators of the operational strength and performance of its business by identifying those items that are not directly a reflection of business performance or indicative of ongoing business trends. In addition, these measures allow management to assess Liberty Capital’s performance, its ability to service its debt, fund operations and make additional investments with internally generated funds, perform analytical comparisons, and identify strategies to improve performance. Liberty Capital believes presenting free cash flow on a trailing twelve month basis more accurately demonstrates the company’s liquidity profile by minimizing seasonal fluctuations, particularly around timing of Universal Service Fund cash receipts.  Because Adjusted OIBDA and free cash flow are used as measures of operating performance and liquidity, respectively, Liberty Capital views operating income and net cash provided by operating activities, respectively, as the most directly comparable GAAP measures. Adjusted OIBDA and free cash flow are not meant to replace or supersede operating income, net cash provided by operating activities or any other GAAP measure, but rather to supplement such GAAP measures in order to present investors with the same information that Liberty Capital’s management considers in assessing the results of operations and performance of its assets.
