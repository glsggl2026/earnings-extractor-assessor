Cushman & Wakefield Reports Financial Results for the Third Quarter 2026

NEW YORK (BUSINESS WIRE), November 4, 2026 — Cushman & Wakefield Ltd. (NYSE: CWK) today reported financial results for the third quarter of 2026. Diluted earnings per share (“EPS”) was $0.22, flat with prior year, while Adjusted EPS was $0.37, up 28%.

“Today we reported our seventh consecutive quarter of double-digit adjusted earnings per share growth, showcasing the power of our balanced platform and strategy. With $800 million in debt paydowns since 2024 our balance sheet has been transformed, and we enter this next stage of growth with increased strength, stability and optionality,” said Michelle MacKay, Chief Executive Officer of Cushman & Wakefield. “In three years, we have built a more agile and future-ready company, and the opportunity in front of us has never been greater. Our teams have consistently delivered, and we will continue to focus on converting the strength we have built into durable, profitable long-term growth.”

Third Quarter Results (unaudited)
Revenue
Revenue of $2.8 billion increased $201.9 million or 8% compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025, primarily driven by Services, Leasing and Capital markets revenue growth of 5%, 15% and 18%, respectively. Services revenue was strong across all segments, led by higher global facilities management revenue, which increased approximately $53.0 million, including through new client wins and the expansion of existing client mandates, and higher project management revenue, especially in EMEA and APAC, which increased approximately $10.0 million and $8.0 million, respectively. Leasing revenue increased principally driven by broad-based strength in the Americas as demand for high-quality assets remained strong, driving growth across all deal sizes, with continued strength in office and industrial leasing, including data centers. Capital markets revenue increased 18%, primarily due to 24% revenue growth in the Americas, which experienced growth in all major asset classes. Valuation and other revenue was relatively flat.
Costs of services
Costs of services of $2.3 billion increased $169.5 million or 8% compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025. Gross contract costs increased $30.3 million or 3%, principally driven by an increase in reimbursed client-dedicated labor costs, third-party consumables and subcontractor costs. Cost of services provided to clients increased $139.2 million or 13%, primarily due to an increase in employment costs of approximately $119.0 million, including higher commissions associated with higher brokerage revenue and higher salaries as a result of higher Services revenue.
Operating, administrative and other
Operating, administrative and other expenses of $329.6 million increased $9.0 million or 3% compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025, driven by an increase in employment costs of approximately $9.0 million, largely due to higher salaries and bonuses, as well as strategic investments and cost inflation.
Interest expense, net of interest income
Interest expense, net of interest income of $49.2 million decreased $6.8 million or 12% compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025, primarily driven by lower outstanding principal balances on our debt following optional principal prepayments on our term loans made in 2025 and partial redemptions of the senior secured notes due in 2028 (the “2028 Notes”) in 2026, as well as lower interest rates on our term loans compared to the prior period as a result of repricings in 2025 and 2026.
Earnings (loss) from equity method investments
Earnings from equity method investments was $5.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to loss from equity method investments of $8.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The $13.8 million improvement in Earnings (loss) from equity method investments was primarily due to an increase of $13.5 million recognized from our equity method investment in Cushman Wakefield Greystone LLC (the “Greystone JV”) largely driven by lower provisions for credit losses for mortgage loans compared to the third quarter of 2025. In the third quarter of 2026, the Greystone JV recorded a non-cash provision for loan losses of $3.5 million, of which the company recorded $1.4 million based on its 40% equity interest which was included within Earnings (loss) from equity method investments. Changes in expectations and forecasts may materially impact the provision for loan losses in the future.
Other (expense) income, net
Other expense, net was $2.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to other income, net of $2.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The $4.4 million decline was principally driven by a higher net unrealized loss on our real estate investments of $4.8 million.
Provision for (benefit from) income taxes
Provision for income taxes for the third quarter of 2026 was $31.6 million on earnings before income taxes of $83.7 million. For the third quarter of 2025, the benefit from income taxes was $6.3 million on earnings before income taxes of $45.1 million. The change from income tax benefit to income tax expense was primarily attributable to higher earnings before income taxes and changes in the jurisdictional mix of those earnings, as well as favorable discrete tax benefits recognized in the third quarter of 2025, including the non-recurring release of valuation allowances in the United Kingdom (“U.K.”) on certain deferred tax assets.

Net income and Adjusted EBITDA
Net income of $52.1 million increased $0.7 million or 1% compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase in net income was principally driven by growth in our Services, Leasing and Capital markets service lines, lower interest expense and higher earnings from equity method investments, partially offset by a higher net unrealized loss on real estate investments, higher income tax expense, strategic investments and cost inflation.
Adjusted EBITDA of $183.7 million increased $24.1 million or 15% compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025, attributable to the same factors impacting Net income above, with the exception of interest expense, the impact of the Greystone JV, net unrealized losses on real estate investments and income taxes.

Year-to-Date Results (unaudited)
Revenue
Revenue of $8.1 billion increased $731.8 million or 10% compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025, primarily driven by Services and Leasing revenue growth of 7% and 20%, respectively. Services revenue was strong across all segments, led by higher facilities management revenue, which increased approximately $186.0 million, including through new client wins and the expansion of existing client mandates, and higher project management revenue of approximately $113.0 million. Leasing revenue increased driven by growth in the Americas across all deal sizes, with continued strength in office and industrial leasing, including data centers, as demand for high-quality assets remained strong. Capital markets revenue increased 10%, attributable to growth in the Americas, reflecting our ongoing investments in hiring top talent and strengthening our platform. Valuation and other revenue increased 6%.
Costs of services
Costs of services of $6.7 billion increased $618.3 million or 10% compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Gross contract costs increased $235.6 million or 8%, principally driven by an increase in reimbursed client-dedicated labor costs, third-party consumables and subcontractor costs. Nearly all of Gross contract costs related to Services. Cost of services provided to clients increased $382.7 million or 13%, primarily due to an increase in employment costs of approximately $332.0 million, including higher commissions associated with higher brokerage revenue and higher salaries as a result of higher Services revenue. Of the $382.7 million increase in Cost of services provided to clients, $109.5 million related to Services. Total costs of services as a percentage of total revenue was 82% for both the nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025.
Operating, administrative and other
Operating, administrative and other expenses of $1.0 billion increased $71.0 million or 8% compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025, primarily driven by an increase in employment costs of approximately $37.0 million, largely due to higher salaries, as well as higher technology costs, higher occupancy costs, strategic investments and cost inflation. In addition, the company recorded a non-cash servicing liability of $11.8 million related to the revolving accounts receivables securitization program (the “A/R Securitization”) amendment in March 2026. Operating, administrative and other expenses as a percentage of total revenue was 13% for both the nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025.
Restructuring, impairment and related charges
The company did not incur any Restructuring, impairment and related charges during the nine months ended September 30, 2026. In the nine months ended September 30, 2025, Restructuring, impairment and related charges of $6.5 million were related to an impairment loss on real estate investments.
Interest expense, net of interest income
Interest expense, net of interest income of $157.9 million decreased $3.6 million or 2% compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025, primarily driven by lower outstanding principal balances on our debt following optional principal prepayments on our term loans made in 2025 and partial redemptions of the 2028 Notes in 2026, as well as lower interest rates on our term loans compared to the prior period as a result of repricings in 2025 and 2026. These favorable trends were offset by $4.5 million of new transaction costs related to the refinancing of a portion of the borrowings under the credit agreement in June 2026, as well as a total $2.2 million loss on debt extinguishment from the partial redemptions of the 2028 Notes.

Earnings (loss) from equity method investments
Earnings from equity method investments of $3.4 million increased $0.7 million or 26% compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025, reflecting a $4.5 million improvement in earnings recognized from the Greystone JV, driven primarily by lower provisions for credit losses for mortgage loans compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025. In the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Greystone JV recorded a non-cash provision for loan losses of $21.1 million, of which the company recorded $8.4 million based on its 40% equity interest which was included within Earnings (loss) from equity method investments. Changes in expectations and forecasts may materially impact the provision for loan losses in the future. This trend was partially offset by lower earnings from our equity method investment in CWVS Holding Limited (the “Onewo JV”), which declined $2.6 million compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025, due to higher provisions for credit losses.
Other (expense) income, net
Other expense, net was $16.8 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to other income, net of $9.5 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The $26.3 million change was principally due to the non-cash settlement loss of $17.2 million related to a pension buy-out arrangement in the U.K. In addition, the company recognized a higher net unrealized loss from our real estate investments compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025.
Provision for (benefit from) income taxes
Provision for income taxes for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $59.2 million on earnings before income taxes of $151.4 million. For the nine months ended September 30, 2025, the provision for income taxes was $15.7 million on earnings before income taxes of $126.3 million. The increase in income tax expense compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025 was primarily attributable to higher earnings before income taxes and changes in the jurisdictional mix of those earnings, as well as discrete tax adjustments, including higher accruals associated with uncertain tax positions, return to provision adjustments from various foreign entities and the non-recurring release of valuation allowances in the U.K. on certain deferred tax assets in the third quarter of 2025.
Net income and Adjusted EBITDA
Net income of $92.2 million decreased $18.4 million or 17% compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease in net income was principally driven by the pension buy-out settlement loss, A/R Securitization servicing liability, higher income tax expense, higher technology costs, higher occupancy costs, strategic investments and cost inflation. These unfavorable trends were partially offset by growth in all of our service lines, with particular strength in Services and Leasing.
Adjusted EBITDA of $478.6 million increased $61.1 million or 15% compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025, attributable to the same factors impacting Net income above, with the exception of the pension buy-out settlement loss, the A/R Securitization servicing liability and income taxes.

Balance Sheet
Liquidity as of September 30, 2026 was $1.5 billion, consisting of availability on the company’s undrawn revolving credit facility of $1.0 billion and cash and cash equivalents of $0.5 billion.
As of September 30, 2026, the company had outstanding term loans of $2.0 billion, senior secured notes totaling $0.6 billion and cash and cash equivalents of $0.5 billion, resulting in net debt of $2.1 billion. See the “Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures” section in this release for the definition of, and a description of the purposes for which management uses, this non-GAAP financial measure.
On October 21, 2026, the company fully redeemed the remaining $150.0 million principal amount outstanding of the 2028 Notes, completing the retirement of these notes and eliminating the company’s nearest debt maturity. Following the redemption, there are no long-term debt arrangements maturing prior to 2030.

About Cushman & Wakefield
Cushman & Wakefield (NYSE: CWK) is a leading global commercial real estate services firm for occupiers and investors with approximately 53,000 employees in over 350 offices and nearly 60 countries. In 2025, the firm reported revenue of $10.3 billion across its core service lines of Services, Leasing, Capital markets, and Valuation and other. Built around the belief that Better never settles, the firm receives numerous industry and business accolades for its award-winning culture. Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
The company uses the following measures, which are considered “non-GAAP financial measures” under SEC guidelines:
i.Adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization (“Adjusted EBITDA”);
ii.Adjusted net income and Adjusted earnings per share (“Adjusted EPS”);
iii.Free cash flow;
iv.Local currency; and
v.Net debt.
Management principally uses these non-GAAP financial measures to evaluate operating performance, develop budgets and forecasts, improve comparability of results and assist our investors in analyzing the underlying performance of our business. These measures are not measurements recognized under GAAP. When analyzing our operating results, investors should use these measures in addition to, but not as an alternative for, the most directly comparable financial results calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP. Because the company’s calculation of these non-GAAP financial measures may differ from other companies, our presentation of these measures may not be comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies.
The company believes that these measures provide a more complete understanding of ongoing operations, enhance comparability of current results to prior periods and may be useful for investors to analyze our financial performance. The measures eliminate the impact of certain items that may obscure trends in the underlying performance of our business. The company believes that they are useful to investors for the additional purposes described below.
Adjusted EBITDA: We have determined Adjusted EBITDA to be our primary measure of segment profitability. We believe that investors find this measure useful in comparing our operating performance to that of other companies in our industry because these calculations generally eliminate unrealized loss (gain) on investments, net; impairment of investments; A/R Securitization servicing liability, fees and amortization; system implementation costs; non-cash stock-based compensation; pension buy-out settlement loss; legal settlements and related costs; non-operating items related to the Greystone JV; and other non-recurring items. Adjusted EBITDA also excludes the effects of financings, income taxes and the non-cash accounting effects of depreciation and intangible asset amortization.
Adjusted net income and Adjusted EPS: Management also assesses the profitability of the business using Adjusted net income. We believe that investors find this measure useful in comparing our profitability to that of other companies in our industry because this calculation generally eliminates merger and acquisition related depreciation and amortization; financing and other facility fees; unrealized loss (gain) on investments, net; impairment of investments; A/R Securitization servicing liability, fees and amortization; system implementation costs; non-cash stock-based compensation; pension buy-out settlement loss; legal settlements and related costs; non-operating items related to the Greystone JV; and other non-recurring items. Tax impact of adjusted items reflects management’s estimated annual effective tax rate. The company uses Adjusted EPS as another component when measuring operating performance. Management defines Adjusted EPS as Adjusted net income divided by diluted weighted average shares outstanding.
Free cash flow: Free cash flow is a financial performance metric that is calculated as Net cash provided by operating activities, less capital expenditures (reflected as Payment for property and equipment in the investing activities section of the Condensed Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows).
Local currency: In discussing our results, we refer to percentage changes in local currency. These metrics are calculated by holding foreign currency exchange rates constant in year-over-year comparisons. Management believes that this methodology provides investors with greater visibility into the performance of our business excluding the effect of foreign currency rate fluctuations.
Net debt: Net debt is used as a measure of our liquidity and is calculated as total debt minus cash and cash equivalents.

Adjustments to GAAP Financial Measures Used to Calculate Non-GAAP Financial Measures
During the periods presented in this release, we had the following adjustments:
Unrealized loss (gain) on investments, net represents net unrealized gains and losses on real estate investments.
Impairment of investments reflects certain one-time impairment charges related to investments, equity method investments or other assets.
Servicing liability, fees and amortization reflects the additional non-cash servicing liability accrued in connection with the A/R Securitization amendment in March 2026, net of amortization, along with related fees incurred to execute the amendment. The liability will be amortized through March 2029.
System implementation costs includes costs incurred related to transformative system implementations that may take several years to complete.
Non-cash stock-based compensation reflects a portion of non-cash stock-based compensation expense associated with performance-based equity awards granted to four executive officers in 2024. The long-term incentive awards granted to these four executive officers consisted entirely of performance-based awards in 2024 and they provided for a higher maximum payout than typical awards. This award design structure was unique to 2024. The company therefore excluded a portion of the non-cash stock-based compensation expense associated with those awards from the calculation of its non-GAAP financial measures to improve the comparability of its operating results for the current period to prior and future periods and because the company does not consider it to be a normal, recurring operating expense.
Pension buy-out settlement loss represents the non-cash settlement charge related to a pension buy-out arrangement in the U.K.
Legal settlements and related costs includes estimated losses and one-time settlements for certain legal matters that are not considered ordinary course legal matters given the infrequency of similar cases brought against the company, complexity of the matter, nature of the remedies sought and/or our overall litigation strategy. Accordingly, these losses do not reflect the normal, recurring, cash operating expenses that are necessary to operate our business.
Non-operating items related to the Greystone JV reflects certain non-operating activity presented within Earnings (loss) from equity method investments related to the Greystone JV for (i) gains recognized from the retention of mortgage servicing rights (“MSRs”) upon the origination and sale of mortgage loans, (ii) increases or decreases in the fair value of the MSRs and (iii) estimated provisions for credit losses related to mortgage loans. This activity is specific to the Greystone JV rather than all of the company’s equity method investments based on the Greystone JV’s specialized industry, namely, multifamily lending and loan servicing solutions. Starting in the second quarter of 2025, the company has excluded such activity from the calculation of its non-GAAP financial measures as it is non-cash in nature and does not represent the underlying operating performance of the business. This activity is reported entirely within the Americas reportable segment.
