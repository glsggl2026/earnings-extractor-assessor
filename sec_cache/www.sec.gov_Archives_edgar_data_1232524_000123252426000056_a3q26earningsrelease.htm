Jazz Pharmaceuticals Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

– Broad commercial execution drives record quarterly revenues of $1.2 billion (+10% YoY) –

– Company raises full year 2026 total revenue guidance –

– Ziihera® launched in HER2+ 1L mGEA –

– Xywav® revenues grew 12% YoY with 425 net patient adds in 3Q26 –

– Closed Actio acquisition, adding ABS-1230 (JZP6979) to rare epilepsy portfolio –


DUBLIN, November 2, 2026 -- Jazz Pharmaceuticals plc (Nasdaq: JAZZ) today announced financial results for the third quarter of 2026 (3Q26).

"Our third-quarter performance reflects sustained execution across the business and gives us confidence to raise our full-year total revenue guidance," said Renee Gala, president and chief executive officer of Jazz Pharmaceuticals. "At the same time, we continue to strengthen the drivers of our future growth, with the approval and rapid launch of Ziihera in first-line HER2-positive GEA and the acquisition of Actio, which adds ABS-1230 and deepens our pipeline in rare, refractory epilepsies. Jazz is moving with momentum and urgency to advance practice-changing medicines that deliver meaningful benefit for patients and durable value for shareholders."

Recent Key Highlights

•Achieved total revenues of $1.2 billion in 3Q26, reflecting 10% year-over-year (YoY) growth.
•Received U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) approval and rapidly launched Ziihera-based combinations in first-line (1L) gastroesophageal adenocarcinoma (GEA).
•Presented final analysis from the HERIZON-GEA-01 trial at European Society for Medical Oncology Congress 2026 (ESMO), demonstrating sustained survival benefit at three years. Longer follow up of Ziihera plus tislelizumab and chemotherapy showed an improved overall survival (OS) hazard ratio of 0.65, further supporting Ziihera-based combinations as standard-of-care.
•Closed acquisition of Actio Biosciences, Inc. (Actio), adding ABS-1230 (JZP6979) to rare epilepsy portfolio.
•Generated $1.3 billion in cash from operations in the first nine months of 2026.
•Raised 2026 total revenue guidance to a range of $4.775 - $4.85 billion.

Business Updates

Xywav (calcium, magnesium, potassium, and sodium oxybates) oral solution:
•Xywav net product sales increased 12% YoY to $483 million in 3Q26.
•Robust new patient growth drove revenue, with approximately 425 net patient adds in 3Q26. There were approximately 17,550 active patients exiting the quarter, comprised of approximately 11,375 narcolepsy patients and approximately 6,175 idiopathic hypersomnia patients.
•Continued physician and patient demand for the differentiated benefits of low-sodium Xywav.
1


Epidiolex/Epidyolex® (cannabidiol):
•Epidiolex/Epidyolex net product sales decreased 2% YoY to $295 million in 3Q26. The 3Q26 growth rate was negatively affected by lower U.S. gross-to-net deductions in 3Q25 and lower levels of inventory build in 3Q26 due to wholesaler buying patterns.
•The PDUFA target action date for our New Drug Application (NDA) for the oral cannabidiol capsule formulation is April 16, 2027.

Ziihera (zanidatamab-hrii):
•Ziihera net product sales were $21 million in 3Q26.
•Ziihera received FDA approval on August 25, 2026, and was launched promptly for the first-line treatment of adult patients with HER2-positive (HER2+) unresectable locally advanced or metastatic GEA.
•Promising early demand from first full month of 1L GEA launch with ~40% increase in average demand vials per selling day and a threefold increase in new ordering accounts, 80% of which are community based.
•Positive results from the second and final OS analysis for the HERIZON-GEA-01 trial were presented at ESMO. Mature HERIZON-GEA-01 results showed Ziihera plus chemotherapy delivered statistically significant and clinically meaningful OS benefit. Nearly 40% of patients were alive at three years with Ziihera plus tislelizumab and chemotherapy, irrespective of PD-L1 status, achieving a median OS of 27 months.
•NCCN Clinical Practice Guidelines in Oncology® (NCCN Guidelines®) for Gastric Cancer, and Esophageal and Esophagogastric Junction Cancers, were updated to include Ziihera (zanidatamab-hrii) in combination with chemotherapy, with and without Tevimbra® (tislelizumab-jsgr), as a preferred regimen for first-line treatment of adult patients with HER2+ unresectable locally advanced or metastatic GEA.

Modeyso™ (dordaviprone):
•Modeyso net product sales were $61 million in 3Q26 with approximately 725 patients having received Modeyso from product launch in August 2025 through the end of 3Q26.
•The company anticipates the OS interim analysis for the event-driven Phase 3 ACTION trial in 1H27, based on the current pace of event accrual.

Zepzelca® (lurbinectedin):
•Zepzelca net product sales increased 32% YoY to $104 million in 3Q26, driven by continued uptake of the Zepzelca and atezolizumab combination in the 1L maintenance extensive-stage small-cell lung cancer setting, partially offset by a decline in second-line use.
•On September 30, 2026, the FDA approved the label supplement removing the second-line indication.

Actio Acquisition:
•Completed the Actio acquisition in September 2026, adding ABS-1230 (JZP6979) to rare epilepsy portfolio.
•Potential first-in-class precision therapy for KCNT1-related epilepsy which has demonstrated meaningful seizure reductions in an early clinical proof-of-concept trial in children with KCNT1 epilepsy.
•Currently being evaluated in the ongoing Phase 1b/2a KYRON trial designed to serve as a registrational study to support an NDA submission in the U.S.
•Expect to present initial patient data at a medical meeting in 4Q26.

Capital Structure
In the third quarter of 2026, the company completed a private placement of $1.25 billion aggregate principal amount of 1.875% exchangeable senior notes due 2032 (2032 notes). Concurrent with this transaction, the company repurchased $225 million of its ordinary shares. The company paid for the repurchases with existing cash on hand, and the share repurchases were effected under, and completely exhausted, the company’s share repurchase program announced in July 2024. In October 2026, the board of directors approved a new share repurchase program with no expiration date pursuant to which the company may repurchase its ordinary shares for up to an aggregate purchase price of $500 million, exclusive of any brokerage commissions.
Additionally, in the third quarter, the company amended and extended the company's Term Loan B, reducing the applicable margin by 50 basis points and extending the maturity by five years to May 2033, which is expected to generate cash interest savings and maintain cash reserves.

About Jazz Pharmaceuticals
Jazz Pharmaceuticals plc (NASDAQ: JAZZ) is a global biopharma company whose purpose is to innovate to transform the lives of patients and their families. We are dedicated to developing life-changing medicines for people with rare disease — often with limited or no therapeutic options. We have a diverse portfolio of medicines, including leading therapies addressing epilepsies, cancers and sleep disorders. Our patient-focused and science-driven approach powers pioneering research and development advancements across our robust pipeline of innovative therapeutics. Jazz is headquartered in Dublin, Ireland with research and development laboratories, manufacturing facilities and employees in multiple countries committed to serving patients worldwide.
