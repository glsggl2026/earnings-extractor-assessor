Ainos Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results and Highlights Expanding AI Nose Commercial Momentum

 

Q3 revenue rises 29,567% year over year to $642,889; GAAP net loss narrows 36%

 

$9 million follow-on AI Nose order expands semiconductor relationship; $4.5 million initial payment due within 60 days of signing

 

$175,000 in quarterly recurring revenue expected beginning in Q4 from the initial AI Nose deployment

 

Ainos, Inc. (NASDAQ: AIMD, AIMDW) (“Ainos” or the “Company”) today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, highlighting increased revenue, a narrower net loss and progress in AI Nose semiconductor commercialization and VELDONA licensing. Subsequent to quarter-end, the Company secured an additional $9 million AI Nose order with $4.5 million initial payment due in 60 days, and collected $950,000 contractual payments.

 

Eddy Tsai, Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer, commented:

 

“Our third-quarter results show progress in translating commercial agreements into revenue. Revenue reached $642,889, up 29,567% year over year, driven by VELDONA licensing and our AI Nose semiconductor program. We delivered 1,400 AI Nose systems under our initial $2.1 million agreement and received initial orders from another semiconductor customer.”

 

“Following quarter-end, our existing backend semiconductor customer signed a $9 million purchase agreement for 5,000 additional AI Nose systems—more than four times the value of the initial agreement. We believe this follow-on order is tangible evidence of customer demand for Chemical Intelligence in semiconductor manufacturing. As we execute the agreement and collect contractual payments, we expect it to support revenue growth and strengthen working capital.”

 

“Our focus is on fulfilling customer commitments, expanding recurring services and directing operating resources toward Chemical Intelligence commercialization. Our chemical dataset grew from more than 1 billion records in September to over 3 billion in October, supporting product refinement and broader applications. Our potential $10 million VELDONA licensing framework provides a partner-funded path for assigned clinical and regulatory development while we retain core platform ownership.”

 

 
 

 

Christopher Lee, Chief Financial Officer of Ainos, added:

 

“Our Q3 2026 results demonstrate progress in both revenue generation and operating discipline. GAAP net loss improved 36% year over year while non-GAAP adjusted net loss improved 51%. GAAP gross margin increased to 83.4% from 78.0% in the third quarter of 2025, while non-GAAP gross margin increased to 83.6% from 78.0%, excluding share-based compensation and depreciation and amortization allocated to cost of revenue. GAAP operating expense declined 20.3% year over year. These improvements benefited from the recognition of the one-time $600,000 initial VELDONA license fee and revenue from AI Nose semiconductor programs.”

 

“Beginning in Q4 2026, we expect the $2.1 million AI Nose agreement to contribute approximately $175,000 in recurring revenue per quarter over the three-year service period, providing a more predictable revenue foundation.”

 

“Following quarter-end, we collected $950,000 from the VELDONA license payment and AI Nose semiconductor commercialization. The $9 million follow-on AI Nose agreement provides for a $4.5 million initial payment within 60 days of signing. We have also begun discussions to address convertible-notes due in 2027. We believe these efforts will strengthen our working capital foundation and support the next phase of order execution.”

 

Q3 2026 Highlights

 

●Delivered 1,400 AI Nose systems to backend semiconductor customer under the $2.1 million, three-year subscription services; expecting $175,000 revenues per quarter beginning Q4 2026 over the service period.

 

●Received initial orders from a front-end semiconductor customer.

 

 
 

 

●Introduced second-generation AI Nose, with software and hardware upgrades designed for continuous sensing in industrial environment.

 

●Surpassed 1 billion real-world chemical records, strengthening the data foundation for model development and application-specific validation.

 

●Secured three Japanese patents covering AI Nose technologies and their integration with robotic systems.

 

●Established a potential $10 million VELDONA licensing framework for two initial indications and conditional additional licensing opportunities. The partner funds assigned clinical and regulatory development; Ainos retains core platform ownership and participation in applicable net sublicensing revenue.

 

●Completed VELDONA technology transfer, triggering the $600,000 initial license fee recognized in Q3 and collected after quarter-end.

 

●Launched Atmosphere Engine, a modular platform designed to generate nitrogen and oxygen from ambient air near the point of use for semiconductor and AI infrastructure applications.

 

●Improved financial performance: Q3 2026 revenue rose 29, 567% year over year to $642,889; GAAP gross margin expanded to 83.4% from 78.0%. GAAP net losses improved 36% year over year.

 

Q4 2026 Developments

 

●Signed a $9 million purchase agreement for 5,000 additional AI Nose systems with the existing backend semiconductor customer. A $4.5 million initial payment is due within 60 days of signing, with the balance payable upon completion and delivery of all equipment.

 

●Collected $950,000 in contractual payments, from VELDONA licensing and AI Nose semiconductor commercialization.

 

●Surpassed 3 billion real-world chemical data points, announced October 30, more than tripling September’s milestone and expanding resources for product and application development.

 

 
 

 

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

In addition to results determined in accordance with U.S. GAAP, this press release includes Adjusted Net Loss, a non-GAAP financial measure. Management defines Adjusted Net Loss as net loss, the most directly comparable GAAP measure, adjusted to exclude share-based compensation, depreciation and amortization, and common stock issued as a special stock bonus. Adjusted Net Loss does not exclude interest or income taxes and is not a measure of EBITDA.

 

Management uses Adjusted Net Loss to evaluate trends in cash operating costs. Ainos believes it helps investors assess the cash resources needed to fund its operations. However, share-based compensation is a recurring cost, and Adjusted Net Loss does not reflect equity dilution, cash interest on Ainos’s debt, or capital expenditures. Other companies may calculate similar measures differently. Adjusted Net Loss should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for net loss or other GAAP measures. About Ainos, Inc.

 

Ainos, Inc. (NASDAQ:AIMD) is building Chemical Intelligence for Physical AI, giving machines a new way to understand the physical world by turning chemical signals in the air into digital information. Its AI Nose technology enables intelligent systems to sense and interpret changes in their surroundings, while Atmosphere Engine extends that intelligence toward environmental control. Together, they form a sensing-to-control architecture designed to help machines sense, understand, and ultimately optimize their environments across semiconductor manufacturing, robotics, industrial systems, healthcare environments, and other real-world applications where AI increasingly interacts with the physical world.
