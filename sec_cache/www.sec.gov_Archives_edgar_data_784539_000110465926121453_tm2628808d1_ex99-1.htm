EACO CORPORATION REPORTS RECORD ANNUAL & QUARTERLY NET SALES AND RECORD ANNUAL & QUARTERLY NET INCOME

 

ANAHEIM, CALIFORNIA – EACO Corporation (OTCID:EACO) today reported financial results for its fiscal year ended August 31, 2026.

The Company had 500 sales employees at August 31, 2026, an increase of 43 or 9.4% from the prior year quarter. The Company’s sales force is divided into sales focus teams (SFT’s). The Company had 124 SFT’s as of August 31, 2026, an increase of 8 from the prior year quarter. Management anticipates continued growth in both our headcount and SFT’s in fiscal year 2027. The Company believes it continues to gain market share through its local presence business model.
