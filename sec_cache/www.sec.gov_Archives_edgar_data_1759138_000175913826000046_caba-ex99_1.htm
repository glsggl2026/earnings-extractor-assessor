Cabaletta Bio Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Business Update

 

RESET-Myositis® registrational adult topline results anticipated in 2H27 with BLA submission in 1Q28, including Phase 1/2 juvenile dermatomyositis data for potential PRV eligibility and Phase 1/2 adult durability data

 

Immunomodulator-free durability data from the complete Phase 1/2 dermatomyositis cohort at one year and from the complete Phase 1/2 lupus nephritis cohort with follow-up as long as two years as well as updated safety data from the RESET™ program with preconditioning to be presented during ACR Convergence 2026

 

RESET-SSc® registrational cohort initiated in systemic sclerosis patients with interstitial lung disease including endpoints evaluating improvement of lung function and overall disease

 

PC-free program advancing with maturing data expected to inform 1H27 go-forward clinical development plans

 

Operational runway extended into 1Q28 with $299.4 million cash position as of September 30, 2026

 

PHILADELPHIA, Nov. 05, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Cabaletta Bio, Inc. (Nasdaq: CABA), a late-stage clinical biotechnology company focused on developing and launching curative targeted cell therapies designed specifically for patients with autoimmune diseases, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update.

 

“We are encouraged by the favorable benefit-risk profile observed with rese-cel to date as we made deliberate choices, including construct design, standard and well-characterized manufacturing, and weight-based dosing, from the outset of its development to prioritize patient safety,” said Steven Nichtberger, M.D., Chief Executive Officer of Cabaletta. “We are now seeing increased enrollment and screening activity, with multiple patients enrolled in the past few weeks in the registrational adult myositis study, after a slower period in 3Q26 that we believe was influenced by patient and physician caution following recently reported safety events from others in the field. Additionally, we are excited by progress in our juvenile dermatomyositis program and expect to have a robust dataset from these patients to include with our planned BLA submission in myositis in the first quarter of 2028, potentially enabling our eligibility for a Priority Review Voucher. We also initiated our second registrational study to evaluate the ability of rese-cel to improve lung function and overall disease in systemic sclerosis, a patient population with one of the highest mortality rates among rheumatological conditions. In parallel, our PC-free program continues to build toward a dataset that will guide our path forward on the optimal dose and development strategy, which we plan to announce in the first half of 2027.”

 

Recent Operational Highlights and Anticipated Upcoming Milestones

 

Resecabtagene autoleucel (rese-cel) for autoimmune diseases

Rese-cel is an investigational chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) T cell therapy designed to achieve a potential immune reset in patients living with autoimmune diseases. Following a single, weight-based intravenous infusion after discontinuation of immunomodulators, rese-cel has shown the ability to deliver an immune reset, leading to clinical responses that have sustained over time. Cabaletta is evaluating rese-cel across the REstoring SElf-Tolerance (RESET™) clinical development program, which includes multiple single-arm, company-sponsored trials in rheumatology, neurology, and dermatology with disease-specific cohorts designed to evolve directly into registrational studies. Cabaletta is also advancing a program of innovations to broaden and deepen the treatment potential of rese-cel across autoimmune diseases.

 

RESET-Myositis registrational topline results expected 2H27 to support planned BLA submission in 1Q28

•
The registrational, 17-patient dermatomyositis (DM) and antisynthetase syndrome (ASyS) cohort, which incorporates an outpatient dosing option, is expected to deliver topline results in the second half of 2027 to support a planned Biologics License Application (BLA) submission in myositis to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) in the first quarter of 2028.
•
Based on a recent Type B regulatory interaction with the FDA, Cabaletta has the ability to include multi-year Phase 1/2 myositis durability data to support the planned BLA submission. Cabaletta will present one-year clinical data from the complete Phase 1/2 DM cohort at the upcoming American College of Rheumatology (ACR) Convergence 2026 annual meeting. Cabaletta expects to have three-year durability data from some Phase 1/2 patients at the time of potential launch.
•
The Phase 1/2 juvenile myositis cohort, which is focused on juvenile dermatomyositis (JDM) and incorporates an outpatient dosing option, continues to be open for patient enrollment. As part of its planned BLA submission in myositis, Cabaletta plans to include JDM data from this cohort to support a potential pediatric indication, enabling potential eligibility for a Priority Review Voucher (PRV).

 

RESET-SSc registrational cohort initiated and open for patient enrollment

•
Cabaletta initiated the registrational, approximately 25-patient cohort in systemic sclerosis patients with a focus on interstitial lung disease and an outpatient dosing option. These patients have one of the highest mortality rates among rheumatological conditions and there are currently no FDA-approved therapies that can improve lung function in these patients. The primary endpoint is designed to measure the mean change from baseline in percent-predicted forced vital capacity (FVC) without any immunomodulators or anti-fibrotic medications and on no or a low-dose of steroids.

 

Prioritized PC-free trials generating dose optimization insights for 1H27 go-forward clinical development plans

•
RESET-SLE® continues to be open for enrollment of lupus patients and is currently evaluating higher-dose, preconditioning-free (PC-free) rese-cel. Additionally, RESET-SLE has incorporated adolescent patient enrollment in the Phase 1/2 cohorts with preconditioning.
•
RESET-MG® has initiated PC-free rese-cel dose exploration and is open for enrollment of myasthenia gravis patients.
•
RESET-PV® continues to be open for enrollment of pemphigus vulgaris patients at higher-dose, PC-free rese-cel.

 

Recent external scientific presentations

•
At the European Society of Gene and Cell Therapy 2026 Annual Meeting, Cabaletta presented a poster presentation titled, “Differential macrophage responses to CAR T cell produced cytokines across autoimmune diseases may confer differential risk among indications.” Data in the presentation indicate the potential relationship between the pro-inflammatory activation of monocyte-derived macrophages in different autoimmune diseases, with a focus on Phase 1/2 dermatomyositis and systemic sclerosis patients treated with rese-cel, and the observation of adverse safety events in autoimmune disease patients. In addition, longer-term Phase 1/2 clinical efficacy data in systemic sclerosis patients treated with rese-cel were shared. At one year and off immunomodulators, the five patients with interstitial lung disease achieved a mean percent-predicted FVC improvement of approximately 9% and, among all systemic sclerosis patients with at least one year of follow-up, six of seven (86%) patients achieved rCRISS-25.
•
At the American Association of Neuromuscular & Electrodiagnostic Medicine 2026 Annual Meeting, Cabaletta presented clinical and translational data from the first 14 RESET-MG patients treated with rese-cel and preconditioning. Rese-cel was generally well tolerated, with no cytokine release syndrome in 12 patients and no immune effector cell-associated neurotoxicity syndrome or immune effector cell-associated hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis-like syndrome. At 24 weeks, patients who remained off immunomodulators (n=10) had a mean change from baseline of -7.9 on the Myasthenia Gravis Activities of Daily Living scale (MG-ADL) and -7.8 on the Quantitative Myasthenia Gravis score, and 10 of the 14 patients achieved a clinically meaningful MG-ADL improvement as of latest follow-up (median follow-up of 38 weeks) while off immunomodulators.

Cabaletta will present an oral presentation (0902) on one-year clinical responses in dermatomyositis patients treated with rese-cel at 1:00 p.m. ET on Sunday, November 8, and three poster presentations (1222, 1673, 1728) on updated rese-cel clinical and safety data and the opportunity of CAR T cell therapy in autoimmune diseases from 10:30 a.m. – 12:30 p.m. ET on Monday, November 9.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

•
Research and development expenses were $47.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $39.8 million for the same period in 2025.
•
General and administrative expenses were $7.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $6.8 million for the same period in 2025.
•
As of September 30, 2026, Cabaletta had cash and cash equivalents of approximately $299.4 million, inclusive of approximately $115.0 million from exercises of common stock warrants received between July 1, 2026, and the warrants’ expiration on September 12, 2026. The Company expects that its cash position as of September 30, 2026, will enable it to fund its operating plan into the first quarter of 2028.

 

About Cabaletta Bio

Cabaletta Bio (Nasdaq: CABA) is a late-stage clinical biotechnology company focused on developing and launching curative targeted cell therapies designed specifically for patients with autoimmune diseases. Cabaletta’s lead product candidate, resecabtagene autoleucel (rese-cel), is an investigational 4-1BB-containing fully human CD19-CAR T cell therapy being advanced across the REstoring SElf-Tolerance (RESET™) clinical development program spanning multiple therapeutic areas, including rheumatology, neurology, and dermatology. Cabaletta Bio’s headquarters and labs are located in Philadelphia, PA. For more information, visit www.cabalettabio.com and connect with us on LinkedIn.
