NextBoat Inc. Announces Fiscal Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

Third quarter 2026 revenue increased 110.7% year-over-year to $50.6 million

 

Wilmington, NC – ACCESS Newswire – November 16, 2026 – NextBoat Inc. (NYSE American: NXB) (“NextBoat” or the “Company”), a vertically integrated, technology-driven marine platform and one of the largest buyers and sellers of used boats in the United States, today announced financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

2026 Third Quarter Highlights

 

●Revenue increased 110.7% to $50.6 million compared to $24.0 million in the third quarter of 2025
  
●Pre-owned boat sales increased 80.9% to $37.9 million compared to $20.9 million in the third quarter of 2025
  
●New boat sales increased 230.6% to $7.5 million compared to $2.3 million in the third quarter of 2025
  
●Service, parts and other revenue increased to $3.9 million compared to $0.2 million in the third quarter of 2025
  
●Finance income from Azure increased 96.9% to $1.2 million compared to $0.6 million in the third quarter of 2025
  
●Boats sold increased 98.2% to 220 compared to 111 in the third quarter of 2025
  
●Gross profit increased 90.2% to $8.1 million compared to $4.2 million in the third quarter of 2025

 

“The Company’s third quarter of 2026 was another step forward for NextBoat. Revenue more than doubled to $50.6 million, with growth across every division, and we believe that the breadth of that growth tells us our platform is working. The Company made real progress integrating Apex Marine and Bellhart, and we are using the third and fourth quarters of 2026 to optimize our boat inventory so we enter 2027 in a stronger position,” said Jason Ruegg, Founder and President of NextBoat.

 

“My first weeks as CEO have confirmed what drew me to NextBoat: strong brands, a growing broker network and a platform that can buy, sell, finance and service boats at scale. My priority now is execution, including strengthening the core business and driving NextBoat toward sustainable profitability,” added Ross Tannenbaum, Chief Executive Officer of NextBoat.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

Overall, revenue increased by $26.6 million, or 110.7%, to $50.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, from $24.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Revenue increased across all divisions during the quarter, reflecting greater utilization of floorplan financing capacity, expansion of the brokerage networks at Off The Hook and Autograph Yacht Group, and contributions from Azure, our finance division. Apex Marine and Bellhart, both acquired in the second quarter of 2026, also contributed service and new boat revenue. These factors supported higher pre-owned boat sales, increased brokerage transaction volume, and a broader revenue mix across the business.

 

Gross profit increased by $3.8 million, or 90.2%, to $8.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $4.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase was driven primarily by a $1.4 million increase in service, parts and other gross profit, mainly from the service operations of Bellhart and Apex Marine acquired in May 2026, a $1.1 million increase in pre-owned boat gross profit and a $1.0 million increase in new boat gross profit. Gross profit as a percentage of revenue was 15.9% for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to 17.7% for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease was primarily attributable to lower pre-owned boat margins, which declined to 12.9% from 18.2% in the prior-year period, partially offset by higher new boat margins and a greater proportion of higher-margin service, parts and other revenue.

 

Selling, general, and administrative expenses consist primarily of insurance, utilities, and other customary operating expenses. SG&A increased $1.9 million to $2.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $0.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase was primarily attributable to the operating cost base of the Apex Marine and Bellhart businesses acquired in May 2026, higher indirect marketing expenses, and higher insurance costs related to increased inventory levels under floorplan financing arrangements, each in line with the Company’s planned business expansion for 2026. Additionally, SG&A included $0.2 million of non-cash cost for the warrants issued to MarineMax, Inc. under the strategic partnership agreement and a $0.6 million accrual for a legal settlement.

 

 
 

 

Salaries and wages expense increased $4.1 million, or 151.7%, to $6.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $2.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Leading into and following our initial public offering, salaries and wages increased as we aligned our compensation with public-company market benchmarks and enhanced retention packages to ensure we can attract, motivate, and retain the talent required to deliver long-term shareholder value, and as we added the employee base of the businesses acquired in May 2026. Further, the Company recognized share-based compensation of $1.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026. These equity awards have several vesting conditions including service-based and performance-based requirements and vest between one and five years.

 

Interest expense, net increased $0.6 million, or 123.3%, to $1.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $0.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025, driven primarily by higher average floor plan borrowings outstanding during the quarter and by acquisition-related debt assumed and issued in connection with the Apex Marine and Bellhart transactions.

 

The Company recorded a net loss of $4.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to a net loss of $0.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

 

Adjusted EBITDA1 was a loss of $1.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to income of $0.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025, a decrease of $1.7 million.

 

Fiscal 2026 Guidance

 

For full year 2026, the Company maintains its revenue guidance in a range of $165 million to $170 million.

About NextBoat Inc.

 

Founded in 2012, NextBoat Inc., previously known as Off The Hook YS Inc., is a vertically integrated, technology-driven marine platform transforming how boats are bought, sold, financed, and serviced across the United States. Through proprietary technology, transaction data, financing capabilities, and a growing national acquisition network, the Company operates across boat brokerage, wholesale inventory acquisition, auctions, financing, and marine services. NextBoat’s ecosystem includes Off The Hook Yachts, Autograph Yacht Group, Azure Funding, and proprietary lead-generation platforms. Headquartered in Wilmington, North Carolina, NextBoat is rapidly expanding its national footprint and market share within the $57 billion U.S. marine industry.

These non-GAAP financial measures are not based on any standardized methodology prescribed by GAAP and are not necessarily comparable to similarly titled measures presented by other companies.

 

NextBoat is presenting these non-GAAP financial measures to assist investors in seeing NextBoat’s operating results through the eyes of management and because NextBoat believes that these measures provide a useful tool for investors to use in assessing NextBoat’s operating performance against prior period operating results and against business objectives. NextBoat uses non-GAAP financial measures to evaluate its operating results and for financial and operational decision-making. Reconciliations of the non-GAAP financial measures presented to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures are included in the tables below.
