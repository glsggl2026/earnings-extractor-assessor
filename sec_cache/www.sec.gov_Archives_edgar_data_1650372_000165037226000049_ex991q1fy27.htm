Atlassian Announces First Quarter Fiscal Year 2027 Results
Revenue of $1,772 million, up 24% year-over-year
Cloud revenue of $1,302 million, up 31% year-over-year
Subscription ARR of $6,812 million, up 23% year-over-year
Remaining performance obligations of $4,746 million, up 44% year-over-year
GAAP operating margin of 11% and non-GAAP operating margin of 32%
TEAM Anywhere/San Francisco (November 5, 2026) — Atlassian Corporation (NASDAQ: TEAM), a leading provider of AI-powered collaboration and team productivity software, today announced financial results for its first quarter ended September 30, 2026. A shareholder letter was posted on the Investor Relations section of Atlassian’s website at https://investors.atlassian.com.
"Q1 was a strong start to fiscal 2027, with RPO up 44% and Teamwork Collection customers up 7x year-over-year," said Mike Cannon-Brookes, Atlassian’s CEO and Co‑Founder. "Enterprise AI has hit an inflection point. Model power is table stakes. The advantage goes to platforms that bring people and AI agents together with shared context, deep collaboration, and confidence in the outcome. That’s what we’ve built. Our Teamwork Graph gives agents the context of how work actually gets done, and AMP brings them into teams as accountable teammates. We’ve never been better positioned, and we’re just getting started."
"Subscription ARR grew 23% year-over-year and Cloud revenue grew 31% year-over-year, carrying forward our strong momentum across our strategic priorities of Enterprise, AI, and the System of Work. Customers are increasingly bringing teams, agents, and workflows onto the Atlassian platform as the Teamwork Graph provides unmatched context, enabling organizations to leverage the full potential of AI at enterprise scale,” said James Chuong, Atlassian’s CFO. "We paired that growth with continued operational discipline, expanding GAAP operating margin to 11% while generating $463 million in free cash flow, reflecting our commitment to durable, profitable growth."
First Quarter Fiscal Year 2027 Financial Highlights:
On a GAAP basis, Atlassian reported: 
•Revenue: Total revenue was $1,772 million for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027, up 24% from $1,433 million for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026.
•Operating Income (Loss) and Operating Margin: Operating income was $189 million for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027, compared with operating loss of $96 million for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026. Operating margin was 11% for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027, compared with (7%) for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026.
•Net Income (Loss) and Net Income (Loss) Per Diluted Share: Net income was $91 million for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027, compared with net loss of $52 million for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026. Net income per diluted share was $0.35 for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027, compared with net loss per diluted share of $0.20 for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026.
•Balance Sheet: Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 totaled $1,121 million.
On a non-GAAP basis, Atlassian reported: 
•Operating Income and Operating Margin: Operating income was $576 million for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027, compared with operating income of $323 million for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026. Operating margin was 32% for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027, compared with 23% for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026.
•Net Income and Net Income Per Diluted Share: Net income was $423 million for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027, compared with net income of $276 million for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026. Net income per diluted share was $1.65 for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027, compared with net income per diluted share of $1.04 for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026.
•Free Cash Flow: Cash flow from operations was $469 million and free cash flow was $463 million for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027. Free cash flow margin for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 was 26%.

Recent Business Highlights:
•Service Collection Surpasses 70,000 Customers: Demonstrating strong momentum across enterprise service management, Service Collection has over $1 billion in ARR that is growing north of 30% year-over-year and has expanded to more than 70,000 customers. This growth is driven by organizations expanding beyond IT into HR, legal, finance, customer service, and marketing teams, alongside strong momentum in competitive displacements where seat growth exceeded 40% year-over-year.
•Team’26 Europe: Atlassian held its largest Team Europe event to date in Amsterdam. Thousands of customers and partners attended to discover the latest product announcements and innovations, including:
◦Agentic Multiplayer Protocol (AMP): Atlassian’s foundation for human-agent teamwork moves AI out of isolated, single-player chat windows and into multiplayer mode, where agents collaborate alongside humans as visible teammates. Each agent gets a distinct identity and governed authority, is grounded in the shared context of the Teamwork Graph, and provides clear ownership and a full audit trail for human review. Agents join work the same way a colleague would: being @mentioned, commenting, co-editing a page live, being assigned a Jira work item, taking a step in an automated workflow, or responding to a Loom video.
◦A Rebuilt Atlassian MCP Server: Atlassian’s MCP Server has been redesigned from the ground up, reaching 220+ tools across Jira, Confluence, Loom, Bitbucket and more through a lean interface, covering more ground while using 25% fewer tokens compared to the previous version for the same Jira and Confluence work. With over 2 million monthly users and more than 15 million daily tool calls, it is currently one of Anthropic’s top used MCP servers.
◦Increased Governance: Atlassian showed how enterprises can move AI from experimentation to governed execution: agents get their own identities, scoped permissions and audit trails, all running on the permission-aware Teamwork Graph. That trust layer is what enables large, regulated customers to scale AI on the Atlassian platform with confidence. It makes Atlassian the system of work for both humans and agents, and it deepens the company’s position in the enterprise.
◦Expanded strategic partnership with OpenAI: Atlassian and OpenAI are grounding frontier intelligence in enterprise context, pairing OpenAI’s models with the Atlassian Teamwork Graph so teams can connect ChatGPT and Codex to the real shape of their work, with human review and measurable impact at the center. This partnership accelerates Atlassian’s position as the context layer that helps enterprises realize tangible ROI from their AI spend.
•Further development across the AI-Native Software Development Lifecycle: Atlassian introduced new capabilities that help engineering organizations move from experimenting with AI to using it across the entire software development process. These include Code Context, which gives teams a shared understanding of their codebase and the work connected to it, Jira Agents Dashboard, which shows agent adoption across teams, and DX AI Measurement, which shows leaders where AI is delivering ROI.
•Accelerating public sector roadmap: Atlassian announced its intent to accelerate its IL5 submission forward by six months to July 2028, with FedRAMP High following in January 2029, signaling a commitment to serve public sector customers on the Atlassian Cloud platform. Rovo will also be submitted for FedRAMP Moderate authorization in Atlassian Government Cloud by June 2027. Additionally, Atlassian entered a new OneGov agreement with the U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) to help federal agencies modernize and transition from Data Center to Atlassian Government Cloud.
•A Leader in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Collaborative Work Management: Atlassian was recognized as a Leader for the second consecutive year in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for Collaborative Work Management¹. We believe Atlassian’s Collaborative Work Management solution, which unifies teams across Jira, Confluence, Loom, and Rovo through the Teamwork Graph, is the right foundation for how organizations work together. Teamwork Collection is grounded in deep organizational context, Atlassian's AI is designed to understand the people, priorities, and purpose behind each initiative, enabling organizations to align execution with strategy, proactively resolve bottlenecks, and accelerate time-to-value across every department.
•A Leader in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Marketing Work Management Platforms: Atlassian was named a Leader in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for Marketing Work Management Platforms for 3 years in a row2. We believe Atlassian’s Teamwork Collection is the engine that drives cross-functional marketing initiatives, allowing marketing teams to use Jira to orchestrate projects across functions, Confluence to develop creative briefs and marketing plans, Loom for asynchronous video communication, and Rovo to bring AI into their workflows. Atlassian’s solution is built to enable marketing teams to accelerate the launch of products and campaigns, reduce operational complexity, and focus on creativity, strategy, and results.

Subscription ARR
Subscription ARR is the annual recurring revenue from subscription agreements to our Cloud and Data Center offerings at a point in time. It reflects the annualized value of active subscriptions, including recurring revenue from upgrades and add-ons, but excludes one-time fees. For monthly subscriptions, ARR is calculated by multiplying monthly recurring revenue (MRR) by 12. ARR should be viewed independently of revenue and does not represent our revenue under GAAP, as it is an operational metric that can be affected by contract start and end dates, renewal rates, and the timing of invoices.
