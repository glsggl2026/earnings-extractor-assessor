Century Aluminum Company Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

Net sales for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 increased by $209.1 million sequentially primarily driven by higher shipments attributable to the restart of Line 2 at the Grundartangi, Iceland facility returning to near full production during the quarter and favorable realized metal prices.
Century reported Net income attributable to Century of $302.6 million for the third quarter of 2026, a $53.3 million increase sequentially. The increase in net earnings during the third quarter of 2026 was primarily attributable to higher shipments resulting from the restart of line 2 at Grundartangi and favorable realized metal prices, partially offset by a lower gain on insurance proceeds related to Iceland equipment failure and unfavorable power price realization due to extreme hot weather in the third quarter.
Third quarter results were also impacted by $31.0 million of net exceptional items, in particular, $34.5 million of unrealized gains on derivative instruments, net of tax and a $12.7 million gain on insurance proceeds, net of tax, partially offset by $15.1 million related to equipment failures in Iceland, net of tax. Therefore, Century reported an Adjusted net income attributable to Century of $271.6 million for the third quarter of 2026, a $14.3 million increase sequentially.
Adjusted EBITDA attributable to Century for the third quarter of 2026 was $331.6 million. This was an increase of $4.7 million from the prior quarter, mainly from favorable sales volume and mix and realized metal prices, offset by higher operating expenses and unfavorable power price realization.
Century's liquidity position at September 30, 2026 was $1,161.5 million, comprised of cash and cash equivalents of $713.0 million, restricted cash of $41.6 million, and $406.9 million in combined borrowing availability.
Fourth Quarter 2026 Outlook
The Company expects fourth quarter Adjusted EBITDA to range between $275 to $300 million.
About Century Aluminum Company
With its corporate headquarters located in Chicago, IL, Century Aluminum owns and operates primary aluminum smelting facilities in the United States and Iceland and is the majority owner and managing partner of the Jamalco alumina refinery in Jamaica. Visit www.centuryaluminum.com for more information.



Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Adjusted net income (loss), adjusted earnings (loss) per share and adjusted EBITDA are non-GAAP financial measures that management uses to evaluate Century's financial performance. These non-GAAP financial measures facilitate comparisons of this period’s results with prior periods on a consistent basis by excluding items that management does not believe are indicative of Century’s ongoing operating performance and ability to generate cash. Management believes these non-GAAP financial measures enhance an overall understanding of Century’s performance and our investors’ ability to review Century’s business from the same perspective as management. The tables below, under the heading "Reconciliation of Non-GAAP Financial Measures," provide a reconciliation of each non-GAAP financial measure to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure. Non-GAAP financial measures should be viewed in addition to, and not as an alternative for, Century's reported results prepared in accordance with GAAP. In addition, because not all companies use identical calculations, adjusted net income (loss), adjusted earnings (loss) per share and adjusted EBITDA included in this press release may not be comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies. Investors are encouraged to review the reconciliations in conjunction with the presentation of these non-GAAP financial measures.
